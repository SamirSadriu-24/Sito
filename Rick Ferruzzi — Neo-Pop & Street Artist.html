HELLO.I'M RICK.MY ART IS A RIOT.

My art is a high-voltage collision between the grit of the canvas and the glossy saturation of consumer culture. I recycle everyday icons, mass-media imagery, and childhood nostalgia and recontextualize them through a hyper-saturated, ironic lens.

By blending classic Pop Art sensibilities with raw street art techniques — spray paint, stencils, wheatpastes, and screen printing — I strip luxury and commercialism of their power, handing them back to the public. My work does not ask for permission; it demands a conversation. It challenges the viewer to question what we consume, and what we worship.
